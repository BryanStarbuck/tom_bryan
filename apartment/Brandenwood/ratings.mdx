## What the Reviews Say

* A 2021 visitor on Caring.com gave 5 stars, highlighting management: the manager
  "was thorough and answered all questions."
* Reviewers describe the building as **very clean** with a **sense of community**
  fostered by management.
* The 3.7 Google average suggests some mixed experiences, but with few reviews a
  single unhappy reviewer moves the number a lot — worth reading the individual
  Google reviews and, more importantly, touring in person.

## Interpretation for Tom

Affordable senior tax-credit buildings rarely accumulate many online reviews, so the
signal here is thin. The strongest available signals are positive (clean building,
engaged management). Best verification: tour the building, talk to current residents
in the community room, and ask about staff turnover since the Cirrus management change.
