# Brandenwood Senior Apartments — Overview

**14520 NE 40th St, Bellevue, WA 98007 · (425) 885-6134 · [brandenwood.com](https://www.brandenwood.com/)**

A 60-unit, senior-only (62+), income-restricted independent-living apartment complex in
Bellevue's Crossroads/Bel-Red area, right on the Redmond border — well within Tom's
20-minute-drive target.

## The Bottom Line for Tom

**Tom likely qualifies — but just barely.** Brandenwood is a tax-credit (LIHTC) property
where income limits are *maximums*. The 60% AMI cap for a single person in King County is
about **$66,000** (2025). Tom's **$65,000** income is under it by only ~$1,000. That is a
real risk, not a comfortable fit — any additional countable income (interest, dividends,
pension increases) could push him over. He would not qualify for the cheaper 45% AMI units
(~$49,500 cap). Details: [Income Requirements](./income_requirements.mdx).

## Quick Pros and Cons

**Pros:** Affordable rents for Bellevue; senior-only community; close to Redmond;
elevator, community room, accessible units; near transit with front-door pickup.

**Cons:** Razor-thin income margin at $65k; middling 3.7 Google rating; small units
(600–700 sq ft); older property; limits reset annually so qualification could slip.
