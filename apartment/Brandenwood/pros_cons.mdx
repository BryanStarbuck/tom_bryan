# Pros and Cons — Brandenwood for Tom

## Pros

* **Affordable for the Eastside.** One-bedrooms at $1,179–$1,591 are far below
  Bellevue/Redmond market rates (market 1BRs commonly run $2,000+). If Tom qualifies,
  this is one of the cheapest ways to live in this area.
* **Location.** Bellevue 98007, near the Microsoft Redmond campus and Crossroads —
  comfortably inside Tom's 20-minute-drive-from-Redmond target.
* **Senior-only community (62+).** Quiet building, neighbors in the same life stage,
  community room for socializing.
* **Reasonable move-in costs.** $375 deposit and $25 screening — very low compared to
  market buildings.
* **Practical amenities.** Elevator, on-site laundry, wheelchair-accessible units,
  non-smoking building, transit with front-door pickup.
* **Pet friendly.** Cats and dogs under 25 lbs allowed ($300 deposit).
* **Short wait.** Roughly 1–3 months — fast for income-restricted senior housing,
  where waits are often years.
* **Management signals are positive.** Reviews mention a clean building and helpful,
  thorough management.

## Cons

* **Income cap risk — the big one.** Tom's $65,000 is only ~$1,000 under the 60% AMI
  cap (~$66,000 for one person, 2025). Asset-derived income or any raise could
  disqualify him, and the property will verify everything.
  See [Income Requirements](./income_requirements.mdx).
* **No access to the cheapest units.** The 45% AMI units (cap ~$49,500) are out of
  reach at his income, so he'd pay the upper end of the rent ranges.
* **Middling rating.** 3.7/5 on Google — not bad, but not glowing.
  See [Ratings](./ratings.mdx).
* **Small units.** 600 sq ft (1BR) / 700 sq ft (2BR) — a downsizing adjustment if Tom
  is coming from a house.
* **Independent living only.** No meals, care services, or assisted-living option;
  if Tom's needs grow, he'd have to move again.
* **Only 60 units.** Small inventory means availability can dry up quickly.
* **Recent management change.** Cirrus Asset Management took over from Guardian;
  worth asking residents how the transition has gone.

## Verdict

Strong value-for-location option **if** the income verification clears. The next step
is entirely about the income cap: call (425) 885-6134, confirm the current one-person
60% AMI limit and how income/assets are counted, and only then invest time in a tour.
